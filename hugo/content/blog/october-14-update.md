## What Changed

**Socorro is being sold, and San Antonio is still being built.** On August 11, Rep. Veronica Escobar said DHS will transfer the three [Socorro warehouses](/fights/tx-elpaso-socorro-mega-detention/) "to the U.S. General Services Administration to sell." DHS had bought them for $123 million to convert into detention centers that "could hold more than 8,000 immigrants." The same report says DHS "has not publicly addressed what it intends to do" with the [East San Antonio building](/fights/bexar-county-tx-warehouse-fight/) ([Texas Tribune](https://www.texastribune.org/2026/08/11/texas-ice-immigrant-detention-warehouse-socorro-el-paso/)). ICE paid $66 million for that building and gave GardaWorld a roughly $17 million contract to renovate it. A September 30 deadline for a federal answer to the city's latest inquiry passed without a response. On October 2, Mayor Gina Ortiz Jones said: "I support legal action to halt all activities related to renovating the Eastside facility." No suit has been filed ([TPR](https://www.tpr.org/border-immigration/2026-10-02/san-antonio-mayor-backs-legal-action-to-halt-east-side-ice-facility)).

**Alligator Alcatraz is closed.** On June 25, DeSantis said the site held "zero detainees" and that "The detainees that were here are still in federal custody" ([WFLX](https://www.wflx.com/2026/06/25/gov-ron-desantis-holds-news-conference-alligator-alcatraz/)). The state handed the site back to [Miami-Dade County](/fights/fl-miami-dade-alligator-alcatraz/) in August ([FOX 13](https://www.fox13news.com/news/alligator-alcatraz-site-given-back-miami-dade-county)). A DHS Inspector General report released in September found 79 detainees held for up to two hours at a time in 18-square-foot metal cages, and about 29 square feet per detainee against a 75-square-foot minimum ([WUSF](https://www.wusf.org/politics-issues/2026-09-15/lawmakers-react-to-new-report-outlining-substandard-conditions-for-detainees-alligator-alcatraz)).

**The federal government now owns Otay Mesa.** CoreCivic sold Otay Mesa and California City to the federal government for $1.5 billion in total and still runs both ([NBC San Diego](https://www.nbcsandiego.com/news/local/corecivic-sells-otay-mesa-detention-center-for-billion-dollar-deal/4045434/)). On September 17, [San Diego County](/fights/ca-sandiego-otay-mesa-health-inspection-lawsuit/), which won a court order in June to inspect it, issued a notice of noncompliance because the facility "waited three weeks to report the possible case" of tuberculosis, when state law requires one day ([KPBS](https://www.kpbs.org/news/health/2026/09/17/san-diego-county-threatens-licensing-action-after-otay-mesa-detention-center-conceals-suspected-tb-case)).

**Illinois' siting ban is law.** [HB 5024](/fights/il-statewide-hb5024-detention-location-ban/) bars the federal government from operating a new detention center within 1,500 feet of homes, schools, parks and places of worship. It was signed in August ([Sen. Lightford](https://senatorlightford.com/news/827-lightford-leads-law-barring-federal-immigration-detention-centers-near-schools-homes-and-churches)).

## Notable Signals

**The 287(g) layer is current to September 30.** Prison Policy Initiative updated its [table of local 287(g) agreements](https://www.prisonpolicy.org/blog/2026/02/23/ice_county_collaboration/) in October to ICE data "current as of September 30, 2026." The October 10 refresh added 1,156 agreements to the map. Two top counties moved:

- [Oklahoma County, OK](/county/40109/) rose from 118 to 152 and entered the top 20. The Valley Brook police department (signed June 12) and the Warr Acres police department (signed September 4) are the county's first agreements on the map held by agencies inside the county.
- [Harris County, TX](/county/48201/) rose from 156 to 168 on the Southside Place police department's agreement. We found no confirmed agreement for the Harris County Sheriff's Office.

Six counties reached 100 or more: Greene, MO; Concordia Parish, LA; Howard, TX; Allen Parish, LA; Tarrant, TX; and Coos, NH.

## Coverage Updates

This week's scan added seven agenda items and moved no scores. Five are Oklahoma County jail business. On October 14, commissioners received a report on their annual tour of the county detention center, and the jail trust's citizens advisory board took up a presentation from a detention-center representative and the scheduling of detention-center focus groups. On October 15, the budget board hears an operations update from the [Criminal Justice Authority](/fights/oklahoma-county-jail-bonds-trust-dissolution/). None of the five mentions ICE.
