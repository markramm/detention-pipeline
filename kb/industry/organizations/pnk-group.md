# PNK Group

## Overview

PNK Group is a Russia-founded industrial real estate developer that sold the Social Circle, Georgia warehouse to DHS for **$128,555,500 (about $128.6 million)** in February 2026. Its US affiliate PNK S1 LLC had bought the **vacant land** for $29.4 million in 2023 and built the warehouse; construction cost is undisclosed, so the difference between the two prices is not profit. The company was founded in Saint Petersburg, and its Russian operation partnered with Sberbank Asset Management from 2016 to December 2022.

## Founder: Andrey Sharkov

- Founded PNK Group in **2004 in Saint Petersburg, Russia**
- **ICIJ Offshore Leaks Database**: Sharkov appears as node 20494 — an "officer" in offshore entity records. Specific entity details require direct database lookup at offshoreleaks.icij.org/nodes/20494.
- Claims he is **no longer a Russian citizen** (February 2025 sworn deposition). Lives in New York "most of the time," splits the rest between Monaco and Moscow.
- In **2023**, Sharkov "recalled the brand from Russia" — formally separating the US/EU PNK Group from the Russian PNK Group entity. **This is the same year he bought the Social Circle property.**

## Sberbank Partnership

PNK Group's Russian operations had a documented commercial real-estate fund partnership with **Sberbank Asset Management** from **2016 through at least December 2022**. Sberbank is Russia's largest state-owned bank; it was designated by OFAC as a Specially Designated National with full blocking sanctions on April 6, 2022.

- **2016**: PNK Group Russia's projects were included in Sberbank's industrial property fund
- **July 24, 2020**: Sberbank AM acquired PNK Park Veshki (108,000 sqm near Moscow)
- **December 12, 2022**: a Sberbank fund acquired the PNK Park Rostov-on-Don warehouse, eight months after the SDN designation — the most recent documented transaction
- These transactions were between Russian entities on Russian soil. **No US nexus has been documented** in the Sberbank partnership.
- The Russian PNK entity continued operating as NK Group after the 2023 separation of the Russian and US entities. What the separation transferred, retained or restructured, and whether any Sberbank-linked assets were in scope, has not been publicly documented

## The Social Circle Sale

- **Seller entity**: PNK S1 LLC (Georgia LLC)
- **Property**: 235 acres at 1365 East Hightower Trail, Social Circle, GA — ~1 million sq ft warehouse
- **PNK land purchase (2023)**: $29,392,500 for vacant land (from Walton Leaf LLC, a local developer), per tax records reported by Covington News and AJC
- **County assessment**: $29,786,800 as of January 1, 2025 (Covington News). According to the county's chief appraiser, the building "was not complete and a certificate of occupancy was not issued" at the time of that assessment (AJC, Mar 23, 2026). AJC reported a different figure, about $26 million, for the same assessment.
- **DHS purchase price**: $128,555,500 (paid on or around February 3, 2026 per Bisnow and the court complaint; recording stamp not seen; consideration per Covington News deed reading)
- **Gap between the two prices**: about $99 million between the land price and the sale price. It is not a margin: the 2023 price was for vacant land, PNK then built the roughly one-million-square-foot warehouse, and construction cost is undisclosed.
- **Closing timeline**: city leaders were told on February 4 that the property was in escrow; by February 8 they were told the sale had closed
- **Planned capacity** (DHS plan, since dropped): 5,000-10,000 beds (mega-center) — the city said it could triple the population of Social Circle (pop. 5,400)
- City Manager locked the water meter; the city sued DHS and ICE on May 13–14, 2026
- **Status**: on June 18, 2026 CBS News Atlanta reported that Homeland Security had cancelled the plan for a detention facility in Social Circle, according to city leaders. There is no evidence the building was converted or used. ICE has proposed selling the property as excess, through GSA if no other federal agency wants it (Section 106 letter, October 2026, as reported by Fox 5 Atlanta).

## Scale in Russia

- Developed **33-37 industrial parks** across Russia, Europe, and the US
- **5+ million square meters** (54+ million SF) total
- **Four in-house manufacturing plants** producing building components
- Russia's **leading developer of Class A+ warehouse/logistics** projects
- Uses German modular construction technology

## US Operations

- US presence **since 2017**
- 20+ industrial properties across CT, GA, NC, NJ, PA, TX, VA
- Key complex: **Southern Gateway at Lambert Farms** (McDonough, GA) — 3 million SF speculative industrial park
- Multiple rejected projects: Banks County GA ($325M, rejected 2022), Madison GA (voted down Nov 2023)
- PNK is one of **six companies** targeted by Warren/Raskin investigation (March 29, 2026). **Response deadline: April 13, 2026.**

## Sanctions Status

- Neither PNK Group nor Andrey Sharkov appear on the OFAC SDN list (based on search — direct OFAC tool verification recommended)
- However: the documented 2016–2022 partnership between PNK's Russian operation and Sberbank AM (Russian entities, Russian soil, no documented US nexus) warrants continued scrutiny
- Foreign nationals are **prohibited** from making US political contributions (52 U.S.C. 30121) — Sharkov's citizenship status at time of any donations is relevant
