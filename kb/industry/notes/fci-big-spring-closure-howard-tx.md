# FCI Big Spring closure — do not conflate with Big Spring Correctional Center (GEO)

## Overview

On July 1, 2026, the federal Bureau of Prisons announced it will permanently close the **Federal Correctional Institution, Big Spring (FCI Big Spring)** and its satellite camp, in Howard County, TX — one of six BOP facilities being shuttered nationally (alongside Beaumont, La Tuna, Lexington, Petersburg, and Taft) citing "decades of deferred maintenance and ongoing staffing shortages" and a system-wide ~$4 billion maintenance backlog. This is a **separate, federally-owned-and-operated facility from Big Spring Correctional Center (BSCC)**, the GEO Group-owned four-unit complex tracked separately in this KB (`big-spring-correctional-center-howard-tx`) — both sit on the former Webb Air Force Base grounds, which has caused confusion in press and reference sources, but FCI Big Spring is BOP-run while BSCC is GEO-owned private capacity.

This matters for the pipeline because it is a countervailing signal: while GEO's idle BSCC units are being marketed as reactivation-ready ICE capacity, the federally-run facility in the same county is shedding roughly 617 inmates and 200+ staff positions with no reassignment guarantee — a net loss of federal-corrections employment in Howard County even as the private detention pitch continues. Worth tracking whether displaced FCI Big Spring corrections staff become a recruiting pool GEO or ICE draws on if BSCC is reactivated.

## Key Details

- **Announced**: July 1, 2026 (BOP nationwide restructuring announcement)
- **Facilities affected nationally**: FCI Big Spring + satellite camp, Beaumont, La Tuna, Lexington, Petersburg, Taft (6 closures; 2 additional facilities have operational changes)
- **Population**: ~617 male inmates across FCI Big Spring main facility + satellite camp
- **Staffing impact**: Reduction in force (RIF) triggered; BOP stated employees "will not be reassigned to nearby Bureau of Prisons facilities"; local reporting estimates 200+ jobs lost between Big Spring and La Tuna combined
- **Deadline**: Closure required by March 31, 2027; RIF notices (90-day) can be issued at any time before then
- **Stated rationale**: BOP Director William K. Marshall III cited infrastructure and staffing challenges; part of a broader DOJ/BOP restructuring, not an ICE-related decision
- **Distinct from**: Big Spring Correctional Center (BSCC) — GEO Group's four idle units (Airpark/Cedar Hill/Flightline/Interstate), tracked separately, which remain in GEO's idle-bed inventory marketed for ICE reactivation
