# Florence Service Processing Center — Pinal County, Arizona

## Overview

Florence is in Pinal County, home of the [[eloy-detention-center-pinal-az]] cluster. In September 2026 a $1.2 billion award to GardaWorld Federal Services to expand ICE detention in Florence was reported, the same contractor as the paused [[surprise-az-ice-warehouse-fight]]. Copper Courier quotes an ICE solicitation for work at the 400-bed Florence Service Processing Center, but does not explicitly tie the $1.2 billion award to that solicitation. It matters to the pipeline because it shows expansion shifting from converted warehouses to construction on existing government-run detention sites.

## Key Details

- **Contract.** Copper Courier (Sep 25, 2026): "The Department of Homeland Security signed the $1.2 billion contract with GardaWorld on Sunday, Sept. 21," according to Project Salt Box; KTAR (Oct 13, 2026) says it was reportedly awarded Sept. 20. The Arizona Republic "could not immediately verify" it and it had not appeared on USAspending.gov as of Sept. 21.
- **Scope.** Per the solicitation quoted by Copper Courier: "The project requires the design and construction of a new secure housing unit," to "renovate, repair and expand a 400-bed detention facility in Florence called the Florence Service Processing Center." KJZZ (Sep 24, 2026) says the award is one of "a series of contracts worth up to $7.3 billion to expand detention in five states," that ICE "want[s] these facilities built within one year of the money being allocated," and that planned units are single-story cells for "one to two detainees per room."
- **Local notice.** KTAR reports Florence was not told of the planned expansion.
- **Congressional response.** On Oct 6, 2026 Reps. Stanton, Ansari and Grijalva wrote to GardaWorld's president asking about its qualifications "to manage both projects" (Florence and Surprise), subcontracting and local consultations (KTAR).
- **Measles (July 2026), at a Florence facility.** Arizona Daily Star (Jul 8, 2026) names the "Florence Detention Center"; which of the Florence facilities is meant is not confirmed here. Federal officials confirmed "seven active cases of measles at an Arizona immigration detention center"; ICE Health Services Corps was "ceasing all movement within the facility and quarantining all individuals suspected of making contact with the infected." DHS said non-legal in-person visitation was suspended; the Florence Project said all in-person visits were suspended.

## Unverified

- The contract amount and date rest on Project Salt Box; no ICE/DHS confirmation was found.
- KJZZ's headline refers to "federal land"; the fetched page text did not say what land the Florence work occupies.
