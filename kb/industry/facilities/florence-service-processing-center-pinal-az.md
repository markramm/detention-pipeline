# Florence Service Processing Center — Pinal County, Arizona

## Overview

Florence is in Pinal County, home of the [[eloy-detention-center-pinal-az]] cluster. On September 20, 2026 ICE signed a construction IDIQ with GardaWorld Federal Services for work at Florence, with a ceiling of $1.2 billion; $10,000 of it has been obligated. GardaWorld is the same contractor as the paused [[surprise-az-ice-warehouse-fight]]. Copper Courier quotes an ICE solicitation for work at the 400-bed Florence Service Processing Center, but does not explicitly tie the $1.2 billion award to that solicitation. It matters to the pipeline because it shows expansion shifting from converted warehouses to construction on existing government-run detention sites.

## Key Details

- **Contract.** USAspending lists [IDIQ 70CMSW26D00000014](https://www.usaspending.gov/award/CONT_IDV_70CMSW26D00000014_7012) from ICE to GardaWorld Federal Services LLC, signed Sept. 20, 2026, for a "Single Award Construction Contract (SACC)... designed to support a broad range of construction-related services for Office of Assets and Facility Management (OAFM) at Florence, AZ." Its ceiling (base and all options) is $1.2 billion, with ordering through Sept. 20, 2031. The $1.2 billion is a ceiling, the most ICE may order under the contract. It is not money spent. So far one task order has been placed: [70CMSW26FR0000093](https://www.usaspending.gov/award/CONT_AWD_70CMSW26FR0000093_7012_70CMSW26D00000014_7012), the $10,000 guaranteed minimum, signed Sept. 22, 2026. That $10,000 is all that has been obligated (USAspending, checked Oct 15, 2026). Press reports came first. Copper Courier (Sep 25, 2026): "The Department of Homeland Security signed the $1.2 billion contract with GardaWorld on Sunday, Sept. 21," according to Project Salt Box; KTAR (Oct 13, 2026) says it was reportedly awarded Sept. 20, the signing date USAspending gives.
- **Scope.** Per the solicitation quoted by Copper Courier: "The project requires the design and construction of a new secure housing unit," to "renovate, repair and expand a 400-bed detention facility in Florence called the Florence Service Processing Center." KJZZ (Sep 24, 2026) says the award is one of "a series of contracts worth up to $7.3 billion to expand detention in five states," that ICE "want[s] these facilities built within one year of the money being allocated," and that planned units are single-story cells for "one to two detainees per room."
- **Local notice.** KTAR reports Florence was not told of the planned expansion.
- **Congressional response.** On Oct 6, 2026 Reps. Stanton, Ansari and Grijalva wrote to GardaWorld's president asking about its qualifications "to manage both projects" (Florence and Surprise), subcontracting and local consultations (KTAR).
- **Measles (July 2026), at a Florence facility.** Arizona Daily Star (Jul 8, 2026) names the "Florence Detention Center"; which of the Florence facilities is meant is not confirmed here. Federal officials confirmed "seven active cases of measles at an Arizona immigration detention center"; ICE Health Services Corps was "ceasing all movement within the facility and quarantining all individuals suspected of making contact with the infected." DHS said non-legal in-person visitation was suspended; the Florence Project said all in-person visits were suspended.

## Unverified

- The USAspending record says "Florence, AZ" but does not name the Florence Service Processing Center; the 400-bed scope comes from the solicitation quoted by Copper Courier.
- KJZZ's headline refers to "federal land"; the fetched page text did not say what land the Florence work occupies.
