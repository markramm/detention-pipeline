# Lubbock County: Three Constable 287(g) Task Force Agreements

## The Fight

No opposition is recorded. At its Nov 9, 2026 regular meeting the Commissioners Court took three items, 14.00, 15.00 and 16.00, each to "authorize the County Judge to sign a Memorandum of Agreement 287(g) Task Force Model between the United States Immigration and Customs Enforcement (ICE), a component of the Department of Homeland Security (DHS)" and one Lubbock County constable precinct.

## Key Details

- **Results (Nov 9, 2026):** The county's Legistar record shows all three items as "Passed" with action "approve." Precinct 1 (file 27-0068) and Precinct 2 (file 27-0069): moved by Jason Corley, seconded by Jordan Rackler. Precinct 4 (file 27-0070): moved by Jordan Rackler, seconded by Cary Shaw. The record does not list a vote count, and the official minutes were still in draft.
- **Model:** The Task Force Model lets trained, ICE-certified local officers perform immigration enforcement functions during their regular duties, such as questioning, arrest, detainers and transport, under ICE supervision. In the Brazoria County copy of the form, the local agency bears the personnel costs (see [[brazoria-county-tx-287g-task-force-moa]]). The Lubbock constables' MOA text and any signed copies were not found.
- **Precinct 3:** No item for Constable Precinct 3 appeared on the agenda.
- **Existing agreement:** The Lubbock County Sheriff's Office holds a Jail Enforcement Model 287(g) agreement signed June 8, 2020 (see [[lubbock-county-detention-center-tx]]).
- **State backdrop:** Texas SB8 requires sheriffs of counties with jails to request 287(g) agreements by Dec 1, 2026 (see [[tx-sb8-mandatory-287g-2026]]). The SB8 mandate applies to sheriffs; constable agreements are not part of it, so these three are county choices.

## Why It Matters

Three constable offices, which patrol and serve process on the street rather than run a jail, are being added to a county that already had a jail-based agreement. That extends 287(g) delegation to field enforcement.
