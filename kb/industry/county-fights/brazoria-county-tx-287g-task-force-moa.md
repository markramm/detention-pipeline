# Brazoria County: 287(g) Task Force Model MOA Approved on Consent

## The Fight

There is no recorded fight. The Brazoria County Commissioners Court took up a Sheriff's Office item titled "Memorandum of Agreement (MOA) Between Brazoria County and The United States Immigration and Customs Enforcement (ICE), a Component of the Department of Homeland Security (DHS)" at its Nov 10, 2026 meeting. It sat on the consent agenda as item H.2 and the consent agenda was approved without separate discussion.

## Key Details

- **Vote (Nov 10, 2026):** The minutes record the consent agenda as passed. Mover Commissioner Stacy L. Adams, seconder Commissioner David R. Linder. Ayes: Judge Sebesta Jr. and Commissioners Burridge, Cade, Adams and Linder (5-0).
- **What the document is:** The attachment to the agenda item is titled "TFM MOA Fillable Aug 2025 Format as of June 2026." It names the Brazoria County Sheriff's Office as the law enforcement agency and is headed "287(g) Task Force Model." Under the form, trained and certified personnel may interrogate people believed to be noncitizens, arrest without a warrant in the categories listed, serve immigration warrants, prepare charging documents, issue detainers and transport arrestees to ICE-approved detention facilities.
- **Costs:** Section X of the form puts the cost of the participating officers' salaries and other personnel expenses on the local agency; ICE pays travel costs for training. The agenda item lists no dollar figure.
- **Term:** The form takes effect on signature of both parties and runs until either party terminates or suspends it. The signed copy and its signing date were not found.
- **Earlier agreement:** ICE's published 287(g) list includes a Warrant Service Officer MOA with the Brazoria County Sheriff's Office, signed Aug 7, 2025. The Nov 10 item adds the broader Task Force Model; whether it replaces or sits beside the WSO agreement is not stated in the records opened.
- **State backdrop:** Texas SB8 requires sheriffs of counties with jails to request a 287(g) agreement, with a Dec 1, 2026 compliance deadline (see [[tx-sb8-mandatory-287g-2026]]).
- **Not a detention-bed contract:** The item is a 287(g) delegation, not an IGSA or bed contract. The county's separate detention-center items (water-supply engineering, closed-session attorney consultations, Nov 2025 to Aug 2026) are agenda titles only and their connection to ICE was not established.

## Why It Matters

Brazoria County moved from the warrant-service model to a task-force delegation without any public discussion on the record, in the window before the SB8 deadline.
