# Freeborn County, Minnesota: ACLU 287(g) Injunction

## The Fight

In March 2025, the Freeborn County sheriff unilaterally entered a 287(g) WSO agreement with ICE. On **December 18, 2025**, the ACLU of Minnesota and pro bono co-counsel Maslon LLP filed suit on behalf of four Freeborn County taxpayers: Leslie Kaup, Jeremy Corey-Gruenes, Jim Margadant, and Daphne Hamborg.

The legal theory: Minnesota law does not authorize sheriffs to hold individuals for ICE after they have been released from state custody, and does not authorize local law enforcement to conduct civil immigration arrests. The taxpayers argued they should not be forced to fund an illegal program.

No preliminary injunction or temporary restraining order appears in the federal remand order (May 22, 2026), the ACLU-MN case page (last updated Dec. 19, 2025) or the news report of the state-court ruling (ABC 6/KAAL, Nov. 2, 2026). The state-court docket itself was not accessed.

## Why This Fight Matters

Freeborn was the first announced taxpayer-standing challenge to a 287(g) agreement in Minnesota. The state court accepted taxpayer standing but held the claims premature, so the theory has not yet produced a ruling on the lawfulness of a sheriff-signed agreement. The seven other Minnesota 287(g) counties (Crow Wing, Kandiyohi, Cass, Itasca, Jackson, Mille Lacs, Sherburne) are not bound by a merits holding either way.

The suit sat alongside AG Ellison's September 19, 2025 letter to the sheriff (agreement violates state law without county board approval; detainer holds violate Minnesota law) and the proposed HF 3060/SF 2973 bill to ban county ICE contracts.

Note: the 287(g) agreement is separate from the IGSA for holding ICE detainees. The 138-bed jail continues to house ICE detainees.

## Procedural Timeline

- **March 26, 2025 (signed):** Sheriff Shea signed the 287(g) agreement. Source: May 22, 2026 federal remand order.
- **September 19, 2025 (letter):** AG Ellison wrote to the sheriff that the agreement violated state law absent county board approval. Source: ABC 6/KAAL, Nov. 2, 2026, describing the court order.
- **October 7, 2025 (board vote):** County board Resolution 25-277 approved the agreement retroactive to March 26, 2025. Source: same.
- **December 18, 2025 (filed):** Suit filed in Freeborn County District Court, No. 24-CV-25-2086.
- **January 20, 2026 (removed):** County, sheriff's office and sheriff removed to D. Minn., No. 26-cv-468, asserting federal-question jurisdiction.
- **May 22, 2026 (remand order):** Judge Laura M. Provinzino granted remand; attorneys' fees denied; the order does not decide lawfulness.
- **State-court ruling, reported November 2, 2026:** ABC 6/KAAL reported a Minnesota judge dismissed the suit as not ripe. The judge found taxpayer standing, but no evidence that employees had completed ICE training, carried out the agreement beyond seeking board approval, or that anyone was unlawfully detained under it. The article does not give the order date. No appeal or refiling was found in news or on the ACLU-MN case page (last updated Dec. 19, 2025). The Minnesota court-records docket (MCRO) was not accessed.
- Earlier versions of this page also cited a March 9, 2026 motion hearing (by Zoom) and judicial recusals; those items were not re-verified this session.
