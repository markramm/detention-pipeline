# Manatee County FL: DHS's 287(g) Showcase Sheriff, and the Withdrawn Nonprofit Immigration-Check Measure

## Why It Matters

Manatee County (Bradenton/Palmetto, Gulf Coast south of Tampa) carries a heat score of 74 driven by ice-contract (16), anc-contract (17), igsa (4), and 287(g) (3) signal density, but had no dedicated county-fight file. Two 2025-2026 developments give it a real story: DHS chose the Sheriff's Office as a national model for federal-local cooperation, while the county commission simultaneously became a site of successful, organized preemptive resistance to a immigration-enforcement-adjacent policy targeting nonprofits.

## The Buildout: Dual 287(g) Agreements

- **Manatee County Sheriff's Office** signed a revised, upgraded 287(g) Task Force Model (TFM) Memorandum of Agreement with ICE on **February 12/26, 2025** (MOA document dated Feb. 26, 2025), replacing an earlier Warrant Service Officer (WSO)-only framework that applied inside the jail. TFM certification allows deputies to question people about immigration status, issue detainers, and initiate civil immigration arrests during routine patrol -- not just at booking.
- **Palmetto Police Department**, an independent municipal agency within the county, entered its own TFM agreement with ICE in **October 2024**, confirmed by Chief Scott Tyler -- giving the county two separate TFM-certified agencies (the heatmap's "287g:3" signal: Sheriff TFM + WSO, Palmetto PD TFM).
- Both agreements sit within Florida's statewide mandate: as of Feb. 26, 2025, all 67 Florida sheriffs had signed 287(g) agreements, making Florida the only state with universal county participation.

## DHS Showcase Visit (October 2025)

DHS Secretary Kristi Noem held a press conference **at the Manatee County Sheriff's Office in Bradenton in October 2025**, explicitly holding up the county as a model for federal-state immigration cooperation and spotlighting four local DHS arrests. This is a notable escalation from routine 287(g) participation to being used as a national messaging site.

## Clarifying the Alligator Alcatraz Cross-Reference

Despite the heatmap's facility cross-reference to Alligator Alcatraz (Florida's flagship detention facility in the Everglades), Manatee County Sheriff's Office **has explicitly stated it has not sent deputies to Alligator Alcatraz or transported immigrants there** for ICE, per a sheriff's office spokesperson -- a contrast reporters drew directly against neighboring Sarasota County, whose sheriff's office has taken a more active operational role. This should be read as county-level restraint on that specific dimension, not as evidence of an operational relationship to that facility.

## Resistance: The Withdrawn Nonprofit Immigration-Verification Measure (April 2026)

Commissioner Amanda Ballard proposed a measure that would have required nonprofits receiving Manatee County government funding to verify that they were not serving undocumented immigrants. At least **86 organizations receive more than $26 million annually** from the county for programs including early childhood development, college readiness, shelters, dental care, sexual assault advocacy, and addiction recovery.

The proposal drew immediate, organized opposition. At the county commission meeting in **early April 2026**, nearly a dozen residents and nonprofit leaders spoke against it, and commissioners themselves showed little support during a lengthy, tense discussion. Kelly Kirschner, chair of UnidosNow, argued the policy would "deputize" nonprofits -- child care providers, tutors, domestic-violence services -- into acting as immigration screeners "without legal standards, without training and without protection."

Ballard withdrew/tabled the item rather than force a vote, citing a desire for more time to work with nonprofit leaders and community stakeholders; commissioners scheduled a follow-up meeting with nonprofit leaders days later to discuss "collaborative" alternatives. As of this research (July 2026), no successor version had passed. This is a preemptive-defense pattern win -- civic pushback stopped a policy before formal enactment -- but the measure is explicitly not dead, only paused.

## Key Actors

- **Manatee County Sheriff's Office** -- upgraded WSO to full TFM 287(g), hosted Noem showcase, but states no operational role transporting to Alligator Alcatraz
- **Palmetto Police Department / Chief Scott Tyler** -- independently TFM-certified since Oct. 2024
- **DHS Secretary Kristi Noem** -- used Manatee as a national model site, Oct. 2025
- **Commissioner Amanda Ballard** -- proposed and then withdrew the nonprofit immigration-verification measure
- **UnidosNow / Kelly Kirschner** -- led organized opposition to the nonprofit measure
