Agenda Item: PROPOSED RESOLUTION HONORING COMMISSIONER BRIDGET DEGNEN WHEREAS, Commissioner Bridget Degnen has served Cook County’s 12th district since 2018. After eight (8) years of service, she will retire from the Cook County Board; and WHEREAS, Commissioner Degnen is a lifelong Chicagoan, growing up in Edgebrook and now living in North Center on the City’s north side with her husband, son and dogs; and WHEREAS, Commissioner Degnen received her undergraduate degree in environmental engineering from University of Dayton, after which she served for over two years in the United States Peace Corps in Mali, West Africa; and WHEREAS, Commissioner Degnen worked as an environmental engineer for almost four years before earning a law degree, after which she worked as an Assistant Attorney General in the Illinois Attorney General’s Office. She then worked for seven years in the private sector as an attorney before returning to work for the State of Illinois as Deputy General Counsel for the Illinois Department of Public Health and as the Deputy Director of Medical Cannabis for the Illinois Department of Financial and Professional Regulation; and WHEREAS, Commissioner Degnen ran for Cook County Commissioner and won in 2018, Since then, she has worked tirelessly to serve the residents of the 12th district with kindness, compassion, and integrity; and WHEREAS, as Chair of the Environment and Sustainability Committee, Commissioner Degnen has pushed the County towards a greener future, cutting carbon emissions, installing solar panels on county assets, and electrifying our internal fleet; and WHEREAS, as Chair of the Audit Committee, Commissioner Degnen has worked closely with the County Auditor and external auditors to push for more transparency and responsible stewardship of taxpayer dollars, making common sense ordinance improvements to the county’s Rules, Audit, Ethics, Procurement, Housing and many other County laws; and WHEREAS, Commissioner Degnen has been a staunch advocate for reproductive healthcare and secured a total of $6 million in funding for wraparound services through the Cook County Health and Hospitals system; and WHEREAS, among her most impactful accomplishments, she passed a resolution in 2024 in support of union labor at Cook County Health, urging leadership to drastically reduce reliance on temporary agency workers, saving over $100 million; and drafted and passed a Resolution requiring a fiscal analysis of an increase to 12-weeks of maternity and paternity leave, ultimately co-sponsoring a policy County-wide to implement the same; and WHEREAS, Commissioner Degnen has overseen the investment of over $4 million dollars of Motor Fuel Tax and ARPA funding into infrastructure improvements in the 12th district spread across 14 aldermanic wards; and WHEREAS, Commissioner Degnen has been a fair, pragmatic, and outspoken member of the Board, never shying away from the hardest conversations. She has been a constant advocate for youth in the County’s Juvenile Temporary Detention Center demanding change and has used her knowledge as both an engineer and attorney to improve county operations and pass many pieces of legislation to make government work better for the people of Cook County; and NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED, that Toni Preckwinkle, President, and Members of the Cook County Board of Commissioners do hereby extend their sincere and grateful appreciation to Commissioner Bridget Degnen for her exemplary career, advocacy, and public service on the Cook County Board of Commissioners and send their best wishes to her for continued success, happiness, and good health in the years to come; and BE IT FURTHER RESOLVED, that this text be spread upon the official proceedings of this Honorable Body, and a suitable copy of same be tendered to Commissioner Bridget Degnen.
