Date: 2026-11-19
Agenda Item: PROPOSED RESOLUTION HONORING COMMISSIONER BRIDGET GAINER FOR HER SERVICE ON THE COOK COUNTY BOARD OF COMMISSIONERS WHEREAS, Bridget Gainer was elected to the Cook County Board of Commissioners in 2010 to represent the 10th District, and has served the residents of Cook County with deep affection and gratitude; and WHEREAS, Cook County gave Commissioner Gainer the opportunity to serve the people of the greatest City in the world; and WHEREAS, The County Board gave Commissioner Gainer the great privilege to help strengthen the safety net that is Cook County and one that serves all: the sick, the imprisoned, the homeless, the unemployed, the undocumented and does so with a focus on the dignity of the individual not just the service to the system; and WHEREAS, Commissioner Gainer also sought to change that system across many areas, including: ·	Changing HIV testing at Cook County Jail to be dignified and private and immediate, allowing those testing positive to receive treatment; and ·	Reforming the treatment of women and mothers at Cook County Jail, ending the practice of separating new mothers awaiting trial from their newborns and securing passage of legislation prohibiting the shackling of detainees while giving birth; and ·	Sponsoring concerts and events at the jail for Christmas and Mothers Day bringing countless elected officials responsible for legislating criminal justice into the jail, often for the first time; and ·	Creating the County’s first Pension Committee in 2010, and since serving as its chair where she led a transparent, public effort for a pension reform solution that was fair to both taxpayers and workers; and ·	Lead the effort to create the Illinois Drivers License for Undocumented people and the County’s sanctuary ordinance; and ·	Created the first Citizen Advisory Board for the Juvenile Temporary Detention Center, to require transparency and accountability, leading to the prohibition of excessive force and solitary confinement and the adoption of restorative justice practices; as well as bringing countless people inside the facility to “Share their Story” and remind the young people they are remembered and; ·	Lastly, in the wake of the great financial crisis, organized homeowners, advocates, community leaders to create the Cook County Land Bank Authority, established by ordinance in 2013 and since then has chaired its Board, guiding its mission to return vacant land to productive use and to dismantle the delinquent property tax sales that were a vestige of red lining and segregation; and WHEREAS, Under her leadership, the Cook County Landbank has created almost 3000 units of housing, built by over 600 neighborhood developers, employing thousands of people and generating over $100MM in property tax revenue, growing the property tax base of Cook County by over $330MM; and WHEREAS, The Cook County Landbank serves 76 of the 128 Cook County municipalities and 61 of the 77 Chicago neighborhoods, and has generated over $2 billion in economic impact. WHEREAS, through this work, Commissioner Gainer has built partnerships among government, business, and community organizations to improve the lives of Cook County residents and insure that everyone can love where they live; and WHEREAS, Commissioner Gainer was only able to accomplish these reforms with the partnership of the tremendous men and women who served on the staff with loyalty, intelligence and a commitment to Chicago and Cook County; and WHEREAS, Commissioner Gainer will greatly treasure and equally miss the camaraderie, dedication and fellowship of her fellow Commissioners and forever admire the commitment to justice, dignity and progress of President Preckwinkle and her team; and WHEREAS, Commissioner Gainer and was motivated into public service by the example of her parents who were dedicated to their family, their faith, their neighborhood and their City and lived that dedication through action and. NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED, by the President and Board of Commissioners of the County of Cook, assembled this 19th day of November, 2026, that the Board recognizes and thanks Commissioner Bridget Gainer for her service to the people of Cook County.
