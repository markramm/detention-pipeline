Potential Fiscal Impact: FY 2027 $762,484.00 Accounts: 11100.1326.15295.521313.00000.00000 Contract Number(s): 1953-18001C Summary: The Office of the Chief Judge, Juvenile Probation and Court Services Department seeks authorization for the Chief Procurement Officer to extend for one-year and increase a contract with Treatment Alternatives for Stronger Communities for a reporting center for court-involved youth as an alternative to being detained in the Cook County Juvenile Temporary Detention Center while their cases are pending in court, to reduce violations of probation or supervision and to help ensure that minors will attend court dates arrest-free. Reporting center facilities for court-involved youth are located throughout Cook County. Direct services are provided seven (7) days a week with morning/day sessions and afternoon/evening sessions, Monday through Friday and morning/day sessions, Saturday and Sunday. The TASC community-based site is located in the Englewood Neighborhood, serving Chicago police districts: 1, 8, 9, 10, 12 and 18. The facility offers a clean, healthy and safe environment; programming provides a wide range of interactive and preventive services to meet and address the current needs of youth through specialized curriculum to all court involved youth. The Department initiated the procurement process within the appropriate timeframe and is actively working in coordination with the Office of the Chief Procurement Office (OCPO) to complete the competitive RFP process. However, due to the anticipated timeline required to complete the remaining procurement activities, including solicitation, evaluation, approvals and contract execution, the new agreement is not expected to be in place prior to the expiration of the current contract. In consultation with Procurement, it was recommended that the Department pursue a one-year extension of the existing contract to allow sufficient time for completion of the RFP process and award of a new agreement. This extension will ensure continuity of critical services and prevent any interruption in service delivery while maintaining compliance with the County’s procurement requirements. The requested one year extension is a temporary measure to bridge the transition between the current contract and the anticipated new agreement resulting from the ongoing competitive procurement process. This contract was awarded through Request for Proposals (RFP) procedures in accordance with Cook County Procurement Code. Treatment Alternatives for Stronger Communities was selected based on established evaluation criteria.
