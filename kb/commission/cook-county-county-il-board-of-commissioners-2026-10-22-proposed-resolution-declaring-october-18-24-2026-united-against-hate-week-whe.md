Agenda Item: PROPOSED RESOLUTION DECLARING OCTOBER 18 - 24, 2026 UNITED AGAINST HATE WEEK WHEREAS, United Against Hate Week is a nationwide call for local action to reject hate of any kind, and a resolve to build a safe and more equitable world together; and WHEREAS, Cook County United Against Hate launched in 2022 to activate individuals and communities to champion justice, report discrimination, and disrupt bullying and injustice everywhere it exists; and WHEREAS, Cook County United Against Hate is growing a diverse coalition of comprised of individuals as well as nearly 100 nonprofits, faith groups, businesses, academic organizations, and governments that publicly denounce bigotry; and WHEREAS, Cook County United Against Hate challenges everyone to foster welcoming communities, free from discrimination; and WHEREAS, Cook County United Against Hate creates opportunities to educate about the impacts of intolerance on ourselves, our neighbors, and our communities; and WHEREAS, the Cook County Sheriff’s Office, Forest Preserves Police, Public Defender, State’s Attorney, and Department of Human Rights support people and communities that are victims or witnesses of hate; and WHEREAS, Cook County is a sanctuary County that welcomes all people regardless of their race, religion, ethnicity, age, gender identity, sexual orientation, ability, class, or other backgrounds; and WHEREAS, hate crimes continue to rise and have more than doubled since 2015; and WHEREAS, nationally, according to the US Department of Justice, 10,881 hate crime incidents were reported in 2025; and WHEREAS, 10,528 single-bias incidents involved 12,967 offenses; and WHEREAS, despite a decline in reported hate crimes as compared to 2024, Black and Brown communities continue to bear the brunt of racially motivated hate and white supremacy; and WHEREAS, the figures in the FBI’s Crime Data Explorer reflect only the tip of the iceberg, as hate crimes are grievously underreported; and WHEREAS, of the 9,596 known hate crime offenders nationally, 56% were White, 20% were Black or African American, and 15% race unknown; and WHEREAS, in Illinois in 2025, 377 hate incidents were reported, with 56% of incidents against a person because of their race or ethnicity; and WHEREAS, according to the National Bullying Center for Education Statistics, one out of five students report being bullied; and WHEREAS, the cycle of violence escalates with each escaped consequence, and hateful rhetoric is radicalized to murderous ends; and WHEREAS, Cook County is steadfast in its commitment to fostering inclusion, where every resident, no matter their background or zip code, can live without fear of discrimination, harassment, or violence; and WHEREAS, creating belonging, inclusive, and welcoming spaces is hard and continuous work; and NOW THEREFORE BE IT RESOLVED, the Cook County Board of Commissioners does hereby declare October 18 - 24, 2026 as United Against Hate Week and recommits to welcoming all people regardless of their race, religion, ethnicity, age, gender identity, sexual orientation, ability, class, or other background; and BE IT FURTHER RESOLVED, the Cook County Board of Commissioners are committed to the Cook County United Against Hate Pledge, which asks us to actively learn from those with different lived experiences, question hateful acts, educate on how to safely interrupt and report hate, and support efforts towards justice; and BE IT FURTHER RESOLVED, during United Against Hate Week, and every week, Cook County encourages residents, governments, and organizations in every community to seek restorative justice to repair the damage of hate, discrimination, bullying, systemic racism, and violence; and BE IT FURTHER RESOLVED, hate against one is hate against all, and hate has no home in Cook County...end
