Agenda Item: PROPOSED RESOLUTION HONORING THE SERVICE OF FRANK J. AGUILAR ON THE COOK COUNTY BOARD OF COMMISSIONERS WHEREAS, Frank J. Aguilar was born in Chicago on November 26, 1960, before moving to Cicero, Illinois, where he has lived his entire life; and WHEREAS, Frank is the son of immigrants from San Luis Potosi, Mexico, who worked multiple jobs to ensure a future for Frank and his siblings; and WHEREAS, Frank is a lifelong learner. He received a Bachelor’s in Philosophy from Loyola University, a Master’s in Public Administration from Roosevelt University, and additional studies at the Art Institute of Chicago and the University of Mexico; and WHEREAS, Frank’s deep roots in his community and unwavering commitment to public service are over four decades long; and WHEREAS, Frank’s journey in public service began as a union organizer for Central States Joint Board Laborers Local Unions, which began his career long fight to support workers and labor; and WHEREAS, Frank expanded educational programs at Morton College as Board Chairman. From 1995 to 2000, Frank served as the Executive Director of the Little Village Chamber of Commerce, helping Latino-owned businesses thrive in Chicago, Cicero and surrounding suburbs; and WHEREAS, Frank was elected to the Illinois General Assembly in 2002. While elected, Frank spearheaded early efforts to give undocumented residents driver’s licenses; and WHEREAS, as the founder of the Cicero Mexican Cultural Committee, Commissioner Aguilar established the annual Mexican Independence Day Parade, Cinco de Mayo Celebrations, the Christmas Posada, Day of the Dead and Day of the Child Festivities, Cinco De Mayo, and the Presidential Cup Soccer Tournament in the Town of Cicero; and WHEREAS, on April 16, 2020, Frank was appointed to the Cook County Board of Commissioners, winning re-election to a full term in 2022; and WHEREAS, as County Commissioner, Frank was the chairman of the Zoning and Building, and Finance Subcommittee on Tax Delinquency committees, while also serving as the vice chairman of the Audit committee; and WHEREAS, Frank was a great friend and partner to the dozens of 16th District mayors and their leadership teams. Elected officials throughout the district knew that Frank would always answer their calls and exhaust every possible pathway to support their initiatives and address their concerns; and WHEREAS, Frank was known to be the most active Commissioner. Always out visiting the 24 towns of the 16th District on his motorcycle, Frank was at countless community events, block parties, business openings, parades and more, rain or shine; and WHEREAS, as commissioner Frank championed and sponsored the legislation that provided the Cook County Sheriff’s department with their first helicopter. From its start in August of 2025 to September of 2026, the helicopter has assisted in 44 firearm recoveries, 225 arrests and 227 vehicle recoveries, while protecting residents and first responders; and WHEREAS, Frank was a top supporter of flood prevention and aid initiatives, working closely with federal, state, and county officials to provide the 16th District with over $290 million in funds to provide relief to residents and businesses after a terrible storm in the summer of 2023; and WHEREAS, Frank and his office were known throughout the county and 16th district for their annual events. These included the Three Kings Day and Day of the Child Toy Drives which provided thousands of toys to children throughout the district, the 16th District Dia de Los Muertos Community Resource and Health Fair, and the Empow“HER” Women’s Brunch which brought together hundreds of women community and elected leaders to celebrate their accomplishments; and WHEREAS, Frank is a lifelong student of the arts. His district office was filled with dozens of paintings and sculptures from Mexican and Mexican American artists, and he was always excited to give guests a tour of all his artwork; and WHEREAS, Frank built a family throughout the dozens of towns of the 16th District and their residents, joining in celebrating holidays, trips to Brookfield Zoo, toy giveaways, and meals at local restaurants, always ready to lend a hand to help solve a problem, or simply stop for a friendly chat; and, NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED, that the work, efforts, and contributions of Commissioner Frank J. Aguilar should be recognized; and NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED, Frank J. Aguilar’s lifelong dedication to serving others has made an invaluable contribution to the residents and communities of Illinois and Cook County; and NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT FURTHER RESOLVED, that the President and the Cook County Board of Commissioners, on behalf of the residents of Cook County, do hereby honor the contributions of Frank J. Aguilar’s tenure as County Commissioner in November of 2026.
